Why we started

The furniture we grew up with was generous, hand-finished and made to be lived on. We wanted Hamilton to have it too.

Türkiye has one of the strongest furniture traditions in the world, from deep-tufted sofas to upholstered beds with storage built in. Finding that quality in Ontario usually meant long waits, unclear prices or ordering from a photo.

So we opened a showroom where you can sit, touch and measure first. We keep the collection small on purpose: every sofa, bed and rug on our floor is one we would put in our own homes.

“A sofa is something you live with for ten years. You should feel sure about it before it comes through your door.”

The Home Centre Green team

Up close

What we check before a piece reaches our floor

  1. Every button anchored, every diamond even. Loose tufts are the first thing to fail, so we pull on them.

  2. We sit on every model. The foam should spring back and keep its shape, not sink after a few minutes.

  3. Textured fabric against smooth upholstery, with seams that run straight and edges that are finished.

  4. Metal legs and trim should be level, firm and finished on every edge, with no sharp corners.

  5. No creaks and no wobble. Joints should feel solid when you lean on an arm or sit on the edge.

Where is your furniture made?

Our collection is made in Türkiye and sold from our showroom in Hamilton, Ontario.

Can I see a piece before I buy it?

Yes. Visit the showroom to sit on the sofas, try the mattresses and see the fabrics and colours in person.

Do you deliver outside Hamilton?

We deliver across Hamilton and nearby areas. Tell us your address and we will confirm the delivery day and cost.

Is assembly included?

Yes. When we deliver, we assemble the piece in the room you choose. Pickup orders are not assembled.
