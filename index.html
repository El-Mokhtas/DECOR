Our story

A greener way to feel at home

Home Center Green was born from a simple idea: a home should feel as calm as a walk through an Ontario forest. We bring together furniture and decor in natural materials — warm woods, soft linens, brushed brass and living greenery — so every piece feels right for years, not seasons.

  • Carefully selected, quality-checked pieces
  • Local delivery & white-glove setup across Ontario
  • Friendly design advice, before and after you buy
